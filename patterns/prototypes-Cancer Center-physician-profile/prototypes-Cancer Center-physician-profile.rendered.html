About Oriana Haran, MD

Oriana Haran, MD, is a double fellowship-trained plastic and reconstructive surgeon, currently serving as an assistant professor in the Division of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery at The University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio. She is the only female plastic surgeon at UT Health San Antonio, uniquely positioned to offer her patients a blend of leading-edge expertise and compassionate, patient-centered care.

Haran specializes in breast reconstruction, providing advanced options that include both implants and autologous tissue (using the patient’s own tissue, such as from the abdomen or inner thighs). She is proficient in advanced microsurgical techniques, including deep inferior epigastric perforator (DIEP), superficial inferior epigastric artery (SIEA), diagonal upper gracilis (DUG) and profunda artery perforator (PAP) flaps, which offer patients natural, long-lasting reconstruction results.

She is also highly skilled in the prevention and treatment of breast cancer-related lymphedema, making her a trusted ally for women undergoing breast cancer treatment. She has a particular interest in addressing complex issues related to implant-based breast reconstruction, such as post-radiation capsular contracture and implant-associated anaplastic large cell lymphoma (ALCL), ensuring that her patients receive the safest and most effective care possible.

Haran is an accomplished academic with a distinguished record of research and publications. Her international training and experience have equipped her to provide her patients with personalized care and innovative solutions. Passionate about empowering women, she offers comprehensive breast reconstruction options tailored to their individual needs and goals, ensuring improved patient outcomes.

Haran is fluent in English, Spanish and Hebrew, allowing her to connect with diverse patients. Outside of work, she enjoys playing tennis, traveling and spending quality time with her daughter.
